Frequently Asked Questions

Will the sessions be recorded?

Yes, all sessions will be recorded and made available based on your ticket tier. Pro and Team passes get lifetime access.

Can I get a refund if I cannot attend?

Full refunds are available up to 14 days before the event starts. After that, you can transfer your ticket to someone else.

Is there a student discount available?

Yes! We offer a 50% discount for regular full-time students. Please reach out via the contact form with your student ID.

Will there be vegetarian/vegan food options?

Absolutely. All In-Person and VIP catering options include diverse dietary selections, including vegetarian, vegan, and gluten-free meals.
